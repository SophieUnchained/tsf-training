What we do

Every garden starts with a visit. We measure the space, check the light and the soil, and ask how you want to use it. Then we draw a simple plan with plant lists and a price.

Most of our gardens are under 50 square metres. Small spaces need careful choices, because every plant is on show all year.

Patios

A patio is often the biggest part of a small garden. We help you choose a size that leaves room for planting, and a surface that drains well and doesn't get slippery.

We also plan where the sun falls at different times of day, so the seating ends up in the right place.

Borders

Good borders look full from spring to autumn. We mix shrubs for structure, perennials for colour and a few bulbs for the early months.

Each plan comes with a planting list and a simple care calendar, so you know what to cut back and when.

Balconies

Balcony gardens need light pots, plants that cope with wind, and a watering plan for hot weeks. We check weight limits before we suggest anything large.

Herbs, small grasses and trailing plants are often the easiest place to start.

How it works

  1. Fill in the contact form with a few details about your garden.
  2. We arrange a visit to measure and take photos.
  3. You get a plan and a price within two weeks.
  4. If you're happy, we book the work in.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do the building work?

We work with local landscapers who build the patio and paths, then we plant the garden ourselves.

How much does a plan cost?

Plans for small gardens start from a fixed fee, which comes off the price if you book the work with us.

Can you work with what I already have?

Yes. We keep healthy plants where we can and move them if they're in the wrong spot.
